# Every day we had the same conversation at home: "What are we eating?" 🍽️

So I handed it to Claude as a weekly routine.


Every Sunday morning an email lands in my inbox, with:

→ 14 meals (6 meat, 6 fish, 2 veggie) sized for our family of 5
→ 1 more exotic dish to break the routine
→ suggestions that follow the season and the time of year (October means caldeirada, chestnuts, soups 🍂)
→ for each recipe: country of origin, time (ideally under 30 min), difficulty, a photo, the equipment needed, step-by-step instructions and doses



The best part is the shopping. At the end of the email comes a single line like:

"pescada mais batata mais cebola mais tomate mais coentros…"

I paste it into the [Continente Siga app](https://www.continente.pt/siga/), it splits the items on its own, I tap "Encomendar online", and the cart is done. ✅


## Prompt

Send an email proposal of meals for the week.
The email should always be in Portuguese (from Portugal).

Propose 14 meals (lunch or dinner - 6 meat, 6 fish, 2 veggie - have and image and doses for 5 people - 2 adults and 1 teenager and 2 kids. Take into account the week of the year to suggest some meals that might have relation with that week/month/season.

Add one extra meal per week which is a bit more exotic.

Presentation of the email for each suggestion:
- Make sure to have the suggestion title as a header in caps (give space above it)
- subtitle with country of origin, and time it takes to cook (and difficulty) - suggest ideally less then 30m
- An image of how it could look - get an image from the name of the dish from the internet - place in the email don't add a link
- the recipe (step by step) - also add a note at the beginning of things required (oven, fridge..) 
- The ingredientes  with doses and link to Continente online to add to basket
- the list per recipe of ingredients formatted with the Portuguese word "mais" (meaning "plus" or "and") connecting the items.

Each subsection should have title in bold.
Keep spacing between suggestions for better readability.

Suggest mostly Portuguese and italian dishes but drop some other options sometimes if the ingredients are easy to buy in Portugal, try to keep healthy but don't worry to sometimes go and suggest less healthy.

At the end it generates a list of  recipe ingredients formatted with the Portuguese word "mais" (meaning "plus" or "and") connecting the items.
Something like:
"massa para lasanha mais carne picada vaca mais polpa de tomate mais queijo mozzarella ralado mais leite"

Then explain the follow up steps at the end of the email:
- Copy to the App: Open the Continente Siga app.
- Add all at once: Go to the Shopping Lists section, select the option to add multiple items via text, and paste the phrase. The app recognizes the word "mais" as a separator and automatically splits the items.
Send to cart: Tap "Encomendar online" (Order online) inside the app to convert the list directly into items in your online shopping cart.

Make the email very eye candy.
